Our Story

Founded to solve a problem most businesses didn't even acknowledge they had.

Renuvia was built to solve a growing problem — companies generating e-waste faster than they can manage it.

What looks like scrap is actually a mix of sensitive data, valuable materials, and compliance risk. Most businesses discovered this too late — after a data breach, a regulatory fine, or a PR disaster.

We step in to handle all of it. Securely. Transparently. At scale.

From collection to certified recycling, every step is handled with precision, compliance, and accountability.

We work with companies that don't have the time, systems, or expertise to deal with electronic waste internally — and can't afford to get it wrong.

Why We Exist

E-waste is mishandled.
Most of the time.

E-waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams in the world, and most of it is still handled incorrectly. For businesses, it's not just about disposal.

01

Data Security

Every device holds data. Even "wiped" drives can be recovered. Proper destruction isn't optional — it's the only option that actually protects you.

02

Legal Compliance

India's e-waste regulations are expanding. Improper disposal carries real legal risk. We make sure you stay ahead of every requirement.

03

Environmental Responsibility

Electronic waste contains toxic materials that contaminate soil and water when improperly discarded. We ensure responsible, certified processing.

We exist to make sure companies don't have to choose between doing it quickly and doing it right. With Renuvia, you get both.

How We Work

Every step is
trackable. Compliant.

01

Understand Your Requirement

We assess your e-waste volume, device types, locations, and any specific compliance or security requirements before anything else.

02

Schedule Pickup

Coordinated pickup slots that work around your operations — no disruption, no delays, full flexibility.

03

Collect and Audit

Every item is inventoried, tagged, and documented at the time of collection. Chain of custody begins immediately.

04

Destroy Sensitive Data

Certified wiping, degaussing, or physical shredding — chosen based on device type and your security requirements.

05

Recycle Through Certified Channels

All material is processed through our network of authorized recycling partners. Nothing goes to informal channels.

06

Provide Full Documentation

Certificates, Form 6 filings, audit trail, and material utilization reports — delivered directly to your compliance team.
